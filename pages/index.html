    About Kelvane Club

    What is Kelvane Club?

    Kelvane Club (kelvane.club) is a free daily news site about artificial intelligence and the AI economy: new AI models, AI stocks and markets, regulation, startup funding, and how small businesses use AI.

    Is Kelvane Club free?

    Yes. Every story, the daily Brief, the AI market tape and the AI glossary are free to read, with no account needed.

    How often is Kelvane Club updated?

    All day. The live wire checks dozens of newsrooms and AI labs every few minutes, and a new edition of The Brief is published every morning.

    Where does Kelvane Club get its news?

    Kelvane Club tracks reporting from trusted outlets and the AI labs themselves, then writes each story up in its own words and names the original source.

    Where can I follow Kelvane Club?

    On the web at kelvane.club and on Instagram at @kelvaneclub.
